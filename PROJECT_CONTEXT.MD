Veilbreak is a game about 3 factions that are fighting over a continet named Auerelia. There are 3 factions, Auerelian Dominion, Ember Concord, and the Hollowborn. Each has their own beliefs that guide their classes and weaponry. Auerelian Dominion is those who believe they must conquer and defend Auerelia for peace and orderly society. Ember Concord believe that the Dominion is dangerous, and are trying to exploit the citizens. The Hollowborn don't care, they just want everything to burn.
